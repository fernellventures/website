Your School Hub

Your School Hub transforms school risk assessments from paper chaos into streamlined digital workflows. Designed for headteachers, safeguarding leads, and MAT leadership teams, it automates review tracking, approval chains, and compliance reporting across all assessment types. Reduce admin burden by 50% while maintaining Ofsted-ready audit trails that keep your school safe and compliant.

  • Automated review tracking
  • Digital approval chains
  • 50% admin time reduction
  • Ofsted-ready audit trails
  • Multi-site MAT support

Indie Schools

Indie Schools is the UK's digital marketplace connecting families with independent schools. Parents discover schools through intelligent filtering, compare fees and curricula transparently, and manage their entire admissions journey in one platform. Schools showcase their unique offerings, receive qualified enquiries, and streamline their admissions pipeline from first contact to placement offer.

  • Intelligent school filtering
  • Transparent fee comparison
  • End-to-end admissions journey
  • Qualified enquiry generation
  • Real-time market analytics
